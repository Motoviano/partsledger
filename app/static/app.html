Competitor prices

The app searches eBay UK for the same part from other sellers: by the OE / part number in your item specifics, or by the title when there isn't one. Prices include postage. Your own three accounts are left out. A suggested price never goes below the price that keeps your minimum profit.

Your listings against other sellers

Check only searches eBay for the listings shown: narrow them first with SKU starts with (several: CHRM, GRLL), the product groups at the top, or Top 50 sellers. Each listing is one eBay search.

Fitment check

Reads each listing's compatibility list (fitment) from eBay and flags: no fitment, a title make that doesn't match the fitment, a title that doesn't name the main fitment, title years that differ from the fitment years, and the same SKU on another listing with more fitment rows (which can be copied over; Undo is on Bulk edit → Recent edits). Listings in categories where eBay doesn't take fitment aren't flagged.

Seller standards

Each account's seller level from eBay, now and as projected for the next monthly evaluation, with the metrics behind it, and how often buyers report "not as described" or "not received" compared with similar sellers. Read from eBay twice a day.

Alerts on this device

A notification on your phone or computer when something sells, a buyer asks a question, a return or case opens, or a seller standards warning appears. Turn it on separately on each phone or computer.

Instant from eBay

Without this, the app looks for new sales every 2 minutes, questions every 10 minutes and returns every 3 hours. With it, eBay tells the app the moment something sells, a question comes in or a return opens, and the alert (and stock sync) follows within seconds.

Devices getting alerts

Each phone or computer chooses what it gets.

Recent alerts

Offers to interested buyers

eBay tells us which listings have interested buyers (watchers, abandoned baskets). One offer goes to all of them, valid for 2 days. The offer price never goes below the price that keeps your minimum profit after COGS, postage, eBay fees and the listing's ad rate.

Listings with interested buyers

Click "Find interested buyers" to ask eBay which listings have them now.

Offers sent

"Sold" counts sales of that listing from the day the offer went out until it ran out (2 days, plus a day for late orders). A sale at the offer price is counted as from the offer; other sales in that time are shown separately.

Your discounts on eBay

Multi-buy, kept up to date

Buyers get money off when they buy more than one of the same listing. The app only includes listings that still leave your minimum profit at the deepest discount, and once a day adds new listings that qualify and takes out ones that stop qualifying.

Click Preview first: it shows which listings go in, and then Start multi-buy can be clicked.

Sale

A % off the price for chosen dates (up to 45 days), shown on eBay as a sale price. Only listings that still leave your minimum profit at the sale price are included. Uses the account filter at the top.

Click Preview first: it shows which listings go in, and then Create sale can be clicked.

Payouts

Tick a payout once you've seen it arrive in the bank. Each payout is split into what made it up; the parts add up to the payout amount.

Where each sales month's money is

Money from a month's sales is paid out later: after eBay's holds, and on your payout schedule. "Money from sales" is sales minus eBay fees, ads, postage labels, refunds and other fees, from your sales data (COGS isn't included; that's your own cost). "Paid out so far" adds up that month's transactions inside your payouts.

Upload reports

eBay Transaction reports, eBay All active listings reports, Amazon Date Range reports, and COGS lists (CSV or Excel with SKU and cost columns).

Drop files here The account is read from the file. Rows already loaded are skipped, so overlapping date ranges are safe.

Upload history

eBay connections

Connected accounts sync every hour: orders, fees, ads, postage labels and refunds (listings and traffic per listing every 6 hours).

Copy listings to another account

Copies title, description, photos, item specifics and every fitment row. Postage, returns and payment use the other account's own policies.

Promoted Listings rates

Upload a file with Account, Item number and Ad rate. Each account gets a General campaign called "Partsledger General"; listings are added to it at their own rate.

eBay call usage today

eBay limits how many calls the app can make each day. "App" limits are shared by all connected accounts (one set of developer keys); some APIs also have a limit per account.
